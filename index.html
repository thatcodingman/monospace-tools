Common questions

Does my data leave my device?

No. Every tool on this page runs entirely client-side — what you paste is processed in your browser and never sent to a server.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no signup, login, or account of any kind. Open a tool and start using it.

Is there a file or input-size limit?

Limits come from your browser's memory, not from us — for typical text, JSON, or UUID batches you won't hit them.

Can I use these tools for work?

Yes. Since nothing you paste is uploaded anywhere, these tools are safe to use with internal or sensitive data.
