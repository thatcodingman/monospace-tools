Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

monospace is built to need as little of your data as possible.

1. What happens to what you paste in

Text you paste into any tool — JSON, Base64, regex test strings — is processed directly in your browser's memory using built-in engines. None of it is ever transmitted, uploaded, or stored on a server. Closing the tab discards everything instantly.

2. UUID generation

UUIDs are generated locally using your browser's cryptographic random number generator. We never see, log, or store generated values.

3. Cookies and analytics

monospace does not currently use cookies, analytics, or any tracking scripts. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what's added and why.

4. Accounts and data storage

There is no account system. We don't store your inputs, outputs, or any identifying information, because none of it is ever sent to us in the first place.

5. Children's privacy

monospace is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect information from anyone, of any age, since no personal information is collected at all.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes — like adding analytics or data collection — will be described plainly here.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@monospacetools.dev.
