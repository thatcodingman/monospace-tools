Cron Expression Explainer

Paste a standard 5-field cron expression and get a plain-English description of when it runs.

cron

Fields, left to right: minute (0-59), hour (0-23), day of month (1-31), month (1-12), day of week (0-6, Sunday = 0 or 7). Use * for "every," commas for lists, hyphens for ranges, and / for step values (e.g. */15).

Parsing happens entirely in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
